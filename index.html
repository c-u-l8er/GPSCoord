What we're building

The coordinate utility the internet deserves.

GPSCoord combines the precision of professional GIS tools with the speed and simplicity of a modern web app. Every location on the planet, every format, every conversion — instantly.

Lookup

Coordinate Finder

Enter any place, address, or landmark and instantly get GPS coordinates in DD, DMS, UTM, MGRS, and Plus Code formats.

Convert

Format Converter

Seamlessly convert between decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, UTM, MGRS, and What3Words. Paste any format, get all the others.

Calculate

Distance & Bearing

Haversine or Vincenty distance between any two points. Get bearing, midpoint, and intermediate waypoints for route planning.

Elevation

Elevation Profiles

SRTM 30m elevation data for any point on Earth. Generate elevation profiles between coordinates for terrain analysis.

Explore

Location Intelligence

Timezone, country, administrative region, nearest city, and surrounding points of interest for any coordinate pair.

Batch

Bulk Processing

Upload a CSV of addresses or coordinates. Get geocoded results, conversions, and elevation data in bulk. Export to any format.
